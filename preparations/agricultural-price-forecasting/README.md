# Agricultural Price Forecasting: Research and Proposal

**Stage:** research and model planning. A completed implementation, measured forecasting performance, and an accepted competition entry have not been verified.

## Ideas I explored

My proposal separates ordinary price dynamics from crop-growth conditions and unusual shocks. The idea is to compare a price-history baseline with additional information blocks rather than assuming that more inputs must help. The planned output includes a price distribution and directional judgments, with calibration and interval behavior evaluated separately from point error. These are design hypotheses, not measured gains. [AGRI-2](EVIDENCE.md#agri-2)

The plan assigns a probabilistic time-series candidate to price-distribution work and a separate decision-model candidate, Jev, to event judgments and directional support. This describes a proposed division of responsibilities; no working integration or suitability for the final competition environment has been demonstrated.

## Planned validation

The design calls for rolling-origin comparisons, inputs aligned to when information became available, block-by-block ablations, and evaluation of both point and distributional outputs. External-feature blocks are intended to be retained only when they improve the relevant comparisons. No competition-data result from that procedure is reported here. [AGRI-2](EVIDENCE.md#agri-2)
